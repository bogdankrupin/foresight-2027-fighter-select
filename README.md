# Foresight 2027: choose your forecaster

An arcade-style fighter-select animation built from the speaker lineup of [Foresight 2027](https://seranking.com/webinars/foresight-2027.html), the online conference on Search, Social and AI by SE Ranking, Planable and SE Visible (Dec 8).

Two players roam the roster, lock in a speaker each, and the screen cuts to a versus card with the real session they share on the agenda.

Open `index.html` in a browser, full screen. It loops on its own.

Speaker photos load from seranking.com.
